🔗 Conectores y frecuencia

AND = y · BUT = pero · BECAUSE = porque · THEN = luego

ALWAYS = siempre · USUALLY = normalmente · SOMETIMES = a veces · NEVER = nunca

I brush my teeth AND I clean my hands.

I exercise BECAUSE I want to be healthy.
